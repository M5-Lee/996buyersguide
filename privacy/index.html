Privacy at 996 Buyer's Guide

Effective: [DATE AT LAUNCH]

996 Buyer's Guide is a free educational guide for buying a Porsche 996. This page explains, in plain English, what we keep when you make an account and what we do with it.

Using the guide without an account

You can use the whole guide without signing in. A demo garage saves only in your own browser on this device. We don't receive it. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

What we keep if you make an account

  • Your sign-in: your email address, and your name if your Google account shares it.
  • What you choose to save: your garage cars, inspection checklists and notes, and photos you upload.
  • A phone number, only if you check the shop-contact box and choose to add one.
  • Basic technical logs our hosting needs to keep sign-in working and secure.

Photos

We remove location data (GPS) from photos when you upload them. Try not to upload photos showing license plates, people, or documents you'd rather keep private.

Who can see your stuff

Only you can see your garage, checklists, and photos. We don't browse them. We look only if you ask us for help, or to fix a problem or stop abuse.

Shop contact (optional, off by default)

When you sign up there's a box, unchecked by default: "Doctor Automotive may contact me about my car or maintenance." Only if you check it, we may share your name, email, phone (if you added one), and the cars you mark as yours with Doctor Automotive so they can reach out. Right now nothing is shared with the shop yet; this box saves your choice for when that starts. You can uncheck it any time in your account. Signing up alone never gets you called or emailed by a shop.

What we never do

We don't sell your information. We don't run ads that use it. We don't share it with anyone except what you opt into above, and the service providers that run the site (below).

Service providers

  • Supabase: hosts sign-in, the database, and photo storage. Servers in the US West (Oregon) region.
  • Google: if you sign in with Google.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website itself and may keep basic visitor logs such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts: the site loads its fonts from Google, which can see your device's IP address when fonts load.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from your account page (type DELETE to confirm), or by emailing us. That removes your profile, garage, checklists, photos, and shop-contact choice. Hosting backups may take up to [30] days to clear.

Kids

The guide isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.
